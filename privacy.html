Privacy

Privacy Policy

Framelight has no servers and no account. It collects nothing about you.

Last updated 21 November 2026.

What leaves your Mac

Only what you send to fal.ai, and only when you click to make something (Generate, Make a Take, an edit):

  • your words for the shot or the clip,
  • the pictures and clips you chose for it,
  • a line you recorded or typed for a voice.

Framelight asks before your work goes to fal.ai the first time, and every paid click shows its price first. fal.ai handles what you send under its own terms and privacy policy; you send it with your own fal account. With your key, Framelight also reads your fal balance and the current model prices to show them in the app; that sends none of your work.

What stays on your Mac

Your projects, takes, Library and settings, where you save them. Your fal key is kept in your Mac’s Keychain and sent only to fal.ai.

No tracking

No analytics, no usage data, no advertising identifiers, no third-party code that collects data.

Purchases

The trial and the purchase are handled by Apple through the App Store. Framelight learns only whether the trial was started and whether the app was bought; it never sees your payment details. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Problem reports and crashes

Help ▸ Report a Problem saves a file on your Mac: what you wrote, the app and macOS version, and, if you tick it, the app’s steps and errors from the last hour (no keys, pictures or typed words) and the last crash report. It is sent only if you send it yourself, by email. If you share crash data with developers in your Mac’s settings, Apple passes crash reports to us without your name.

Your rights

Framelight holds no personal data about you, so there is nothing to request, correct or delete with us. For what you sent to fal.ai, contact fal.ai. Under the GDPR you may complain to a data protection authority.
