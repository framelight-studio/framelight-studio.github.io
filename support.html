Support

How can we help?

Write to us

framelight@hotmail.com. For a problem, attach a report: in Framelight choose Help ▸ Report a Problem…, save the report and add it to your email. It holds the app's recent steps and errors, never your fal key, your pictures or the words you typed.

Getting your fal key

  1. Create a free account at fal.ai.
  2. Add credit there. About $10 makes some 250 frames.
  3. At fal.ai/dashboard/keys, create a custom key: the API permissions plus Usage · READ, so Framelight can show your balance. Paste it into Framelight.

fal.ai bills your own account for each picture, clip and sound. Framelight shows the price before every paid click and never sells credits.

Trial and purchase

Framelight is a free download. Making new pictures, clips and sounds is free to try for 7 days; then one purchase unlocks it for good, with no subscription. Planning, importing, playing and saving your film as a video always work, bought or not.

Bought on another Mac? Open Framelight ▸ Settings ▸ General and choose Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Common problems

Generate says “Low balance”. Your fal account is nearly empty; add credit at fal.ai.

fal didn't accept my key. Paste it again without spaces, or create a new key as above.

The balance doesn't show. The key needs Usage · READ; create a custom key as above and paste it in Settings ▸ Providers.

Where are my projects? Where you saved them, like any document. Framelight keeps nothing on a server.
